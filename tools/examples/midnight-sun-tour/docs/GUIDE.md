# Midnight Sun Tour — guide

## Goal
Reach **Turku** before the days run out, with a bus that still runs, a band that still talks to each other, and enough fame, hype and
cash to make **Ruisrock** a success. Your final rating combines the journey score and the festival result.

## Controls
| Screen | Keys |
|---|---|
| Title | ENTER new game, S load save |
| Overworld | UP/DOWN choose destination, ENTER drive, O cheat/minigame menu |
| City menu | UP/DOWN + ENTER, or 1-9; ESC leaves |
| Tour Office | TAB or 1-4 tabs, UP/DOWN select, ENTER buy, LEFT/RIGHT change merch price, ESC back |
| Minigames | each one shows its own keys; mostly arrows/WASD, SPACE, ENTER |
| Game over | R restarts |

## The loop
1. **Pack the bus** (Tetris-style). Missing mandatory gear costs you at gigs. Pack the *Merch Box* to carry more merch.
2. **Drive** from town to town. Every leg costs gas, food, bus condition and a **crew per diem** (8 FIM per band member). Roads may throw
   an encounter (24 of them, each once per run), a moose dodge, night driving, or a runner when the bus is nearly dead.
3. **In town**: rest (tent minigame), refuel (stop-the-needle), buy food, repair, play a **gig** (sound check + rhythm), visit the arcade,
   or open the **Tour Office**.
4. **Ruisrock**: arriving in Turku starts the three-day festival management sim. Your cash, hype, **fame**, merch and upgrades all feed it.

## Tour Office (tycoon)
- **Garage** (towns with a mechanic): Tuned Engine (-12% gas), Winter Tires (-15% wear), Long-range Tank (+20 gas), Sauna Module (+4 morale
  per rest), Big PA (+10% gig fee). Levels stack.
- **Merch**: buy stock in batches of 10 (T-shirts, cassettes, stickers), set your own price with LEFT/RIGHT. Cheap sells more, dear earns
  more per piece. Stock is sold to the crowd after every gig; what is left is sold at Ruisrock for the base price. Space is 24 slots, or 80
  with the Merch Box packed.
- **Promo** (towns with a venue): posters, radio, newspaper — each once per town. Adds hype, fame and a bonus on the next gig fee.
- **Ledger**: income and expenses by category, stock value, fame. Fuel, food, repairs, fines and finds show up as "untracked".
- **Fame** (0-100) grows with good gigs, radio and encounters. It raises every gig fee up to +50%, fills the festival field and adds to your score.

## Tips
- Hype fades by 1 each leg; fame does not. Book gigs when hype is high.
- Buy merch before the big venues (Tampere, Jyväskylä), not after.
- Skipping repairs is fine until the bus drops below 15% — then the runner minigame starts.

## Remade activities — native-pixel edition

P pauses every active scene and stops its timers. ENTER skips instruction screens. All remaining
legacy activity screens have been replaced; the original title stays unchanged. The band now uses
coarse, nearest-neighbor pixel portraits, standing/walking frames and animated instrument-playing poses.

| Activity | Controls and goal |
|---|---|

| Petrol Stop | SPACE stops the large pump marker in the copper band. Five fills; good stops earn 10–15 litres, misses still earn four. Actual fuel is added once when you leave. |
| Load the Sauna Bus | ARROWS moves the cargo cursor, TAB selects equipment, R rotates, ENTER places/picks up, ESC departs. Equipment is illustrated directly inside the cargo bay. |

| Four Stakes | Stop four rope-tension pulls with SPACE near the copper band. A better tent improves rest. |
| Woodland Shortcut | UP/DOWN chooses a ferry, food stop or bridge option; ENTER commits. All cash, fuel and food effects are shown first. |

**Tour jobs** in city menus offers Backstage Express and Harbor Rescue. Choose with LEFT/RIGHT before
starting, then use WASD/arrows. Delivery: fetch a case from the bus and take it to the glowing stage.
Rescue: reach the glowing life ring. Avoid the crossing crew/driftwood. Each 45-second shift pays
20 FIM per delivery, up to 180 FIM. Each job pays once per town; repeating it is practice.

## Ruisrock: management and tycoon

The festival is entirely a management game. There are **no rhythm or music minigames at Ruisrock**.
Your band performs automatically. Your decisions determine the crowd, costs, happiness and final rating.

1. Book up to two available artists per day; select the final Mikko row to confirm. Mikko plays free,
   including when you choose no hired acts. Two artists with recordings are offered each day while available.
   Booking fees are paid once. Two acts must fit your combined budget.
2. Press **G** from booking or staffing to open the festival grounds. ARROWS selects a fixed plot;
   ENTER buys its next level; G/ESC returns. Buildings stay for all three days, up to level 3.
3. In staffing, UP/DOWN selects cleaners/security/stage crew; ENTER toggles daily hires. TAB switches
   to ticket pricing; LEFT/RIGHT adjusts price; ENTER opens the gates. The display estimates attendance
   and gate income and shows the weather and grounds upkeep.
4. Each booked artist's own recording plays during their set, followed by Mikko's original performance
   track. Sets last 90 seconds. **N** moves to the next set; ENTER closes the day. You manage the show,
   rather than playing the musicians' notes. Acts without recordings use the generic performance loop.
5. Read the daily report, then reinvest your earnings and plan the next day. Expensive tickets reduce demand;
   rain/wind reduce attendance; capacity limits sales. Understaffing and poor facilities hurt happiness.

| Facility | First build | Daily upkeep per level | Effect per level |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Food stalls | 600 mk | 80 mk | 3 mk per guest, +2 happiness |
| Toilets | 450 mk | 40 mk | Serve 80 more guests before queues |
| Merch pavilion | 750 mk | 60 mk | 4 mk per guest |
| Stage roof / PA | 1000 mk | 100 mk | Weather protection, +3 happiness |
| Camping field | 900 mk | 70 mk | +70 capacity, 2 mk per guest |

Upgrade prices multiply by the new level. Basic capacity is 100 guests. Toilet provision starts at 80.
New buildings reduce cash immediately and count toward the final profit; upkeep is charged once per day.
Festival checkpoint saves store the journey, not an in-progress booking/show; re-entering restarts the festival.


### Walking customers

The live grounds show customers entering at the gate, following connected footpaths, watching sets,
visiting the campsite, shopping for food and merchandise, and queuing for toilets. Moving legs and
varied clothes make the crowd readable. Green dots mean service; amber dots mean a queue. Each visible
customer represents a share of the actual attendance, with up to 60 on screen.

Stall income now comes from completed customer purchases. More service capacity reduces waiting;
closed facilities and long queues lose customers and hurt happiness. Service affects the final daily
report; purchases are added to gate receipts exactly once. Near closing, visitors walk back to the gate.

Press **G during a live show** to build while the customer traffic and artist sets continue. A new
facility starts serving immediately and incurs its daily upkeep; camping capacity helps the next day's
ticket sales. G/ESC returns to the live overview. P pauses the complete simulation.
