T10 3D scene & camera

Project: nord new box3d -t 3d · look at examples/playground-3d.

  1. Ground: entity with mesh shape: ground, size: [20, 0, 20] and a material color: #5e81ac.
  2. A cube at [0, 0.5, 0] (mesh box, castShadow: true).
  3. Sun: entity with light kind: directional, castShadows: true, rotate it [50, -30, 0].
  4. Sky: entity with skybox path: assets/skybox/day.
  5. Camera: entity with camera type: orbit, radius: 8, follow: <cube name>, userControl: true.
  6. Polish: postProcess bloom: true, vignette: 0.3, tonemap: true.
  7. Models: drop assets/packs/kenney-platformer-3d/models/character.glb into your assets/ and add a model.

Reference: 3D games. Next: T11 3D physics (FPS).
