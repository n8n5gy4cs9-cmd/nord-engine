IGaussianSplattingStreamingPart #

export interface IGaussianSplattingStreamingPart

Handle to a region of a compound Gaussian Splatting mesh reserved for dynamic (streamed) content by GaussianSplattingMesh.reserveStreamingPart. It lets a streaming engine populate the region's splats over time and drive which of them are sorted/rendered, while the compound keeps depth-sorting and drawing every part (static + streamed) together in one pass.

Ranges/offsets passed to this handle are LOCAL to the part (0-based within [0, capacity)); the handle translates them to the compound's global atlas coordinates.
