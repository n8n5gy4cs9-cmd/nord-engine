splitPolygon(polygon: CSGPolygon, coplanarFront: CSGPolygon[], coplanarBack: CSGPolygon[], front: CSGPolygon[], back: CSGPolygon[]): void;

Split polygon by this plane if needed, then put the polygon or polygon fragments in the appropriate lists. Coplanar polygons go into either * coplanarFront or coplanarBack depending on their orientation with respect to this plane.
