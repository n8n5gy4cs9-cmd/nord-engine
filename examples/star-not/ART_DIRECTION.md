# Star-Not original artwork
Built-in ImageGen, transparent PNGs copied unchanged into the project. No UQM visual assets used. PNGs are authoritative; `gen_examples.mjs` copies them. The original raw output is 1254×1254; Nord sidecars use 313×313 frames and ignore two leftover border pixels. UI portrait framing uses per-row crop bounds in the CSS icon helper, so busts and smaller resource symbols remain readable.

## Space sheet — exact prompt
Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprites for original small game Star-Not, inspired by retro space adventures, no existing franchise designs. One square perfectly regular 4 by 4 atlas, sixteen equal cells, individual isolated centered subjects with generous transparent gutters. Transparent background, no grid lines, no text or labels. Crisp hand-painted pixel-art style, clean readable silhouettes, lit from upper left. All ships viewed top-down with nose toward top. Row1: blue-and-cream Earth-race explorer ship, angular red-and-copper Mars-race interceptor ship, curved mint-and-gold Venutian ship, small neutral broken metal satellite (not a race). Row2: Sun yellow star disc, Mercury grey rocky globe, Venus pale gold cloudy globe, Earth blue green globe. Row3: Mars rusty globe, Jupiter banded orange white globe, Saturn pale gold globe with rings, Uranus pale cyan globe. Row4: Neptune blue globe, rocky asteroid, tiny luminous projectile, compact orange explosion. Planets round and self-contained, no rings except Saturn; ship exhaust never crosses its cell. Every item fits completely within its equal tile and does not touch any other item. This is game art, not a poster.

## UI sheet — exact initial prompt
Use case: stylized-concept. Production UI sprites for original game Star-Not. Square transparent-background sprite atlas, perfectly regular four by four equal cells, sixteen centered isolated illustrations with wide transparent margins. No text, labels, outlines of grid, or content outside cells. Retro space cockpit style, navy, desaturated teal, warm amber and copper accents, readable thumbnails. Row1: portrait of original Earth-race human woman captain in blue space uniform; portrait of original Mars-race human man with red pressure suit and bronze visor; portrait of original Venutian green humanoid with gentle leaf-like facial crests and gold robes; solar compass icon. Row2: fuel canister icon, crew helmet icon, scrap-metal resource icon, handshake treaty icon. Row3: glowing relay core icon, icy sample vial icon, shield icon, laser cannon icon. Row4: play triangle icon, save floppy icon, speaker icon, home/earth port icon. Portraits are head-and-shoulders busts, no containing frames; do not invent any fourth race. Whole items fit completely inside their tile. Transparent background.
