# Vallout 2: New Kerava — story, quests, endings
**Setting:** Kerava, Finland, 2031. A green light appears over Sampola, aliens land, the army seals the town (roadblocks at Kehä III, no signal, no sausage). Tone: Fallout 2 dark humour with 90s/2000s Finnish flavour (Kummeli, Kossu, lonkero, Nokia 3310, VR trains).

**Hero:** Tero (no surname) wakes on a bench at Kerava Station with a critical hangover and a Nokia 3310. Goal: reach the Klaukkala safe zone.

**Route (world map):** Keravan asema → Kauppakaari/Karuselli → Sampola/Ahjo → Keravanjoki (the Hive, ruled by the Overmind) → Klaukkala gate.

## Main quest — "Get Out of Kerava"
1. Leave the station, find **Rane** in Kauppakaari — he has a moped.
2. Rane needs his **green fuzzy dice** and a **spark plug**; search Sampola.
3. Moped ready — but the river bridge is held by the Overmind: clear the Hive.
4. Ride to Klaukkala.

## Endings
- **Escape** — ride into Klaukkala on Rane's moped with the dice on the mirror ("Asiaa."). Walking in without the moped gives the sausage variant.
- **Victory (Kerava Liberated)** — the 30th alien drops, the hive shrieks, the mothership folds into the sky.
- **Death** — "Hei hei."
