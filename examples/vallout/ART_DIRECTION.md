# Vallout — quarantined Kerava art

Original raster artwork made with the built-in ImageGen tool (2026-10-13). User-provided Fallout screenshots guide the 1998 pre-rendered aesthetic; user-provided Kerava photographs guide architecture. Reference images are not bundled. No original Fallout graphics are shipped.

Desaturated ash, concrete, olive steel and rust. Kerava's yellow timber station, red/silver commuter train, blue shopping arcade, balconies, birches and globe lamps carry the location. Interfaces use scratched metal, recessed green CRTs, amber labels and red lamps. Existing filenames, frame names and combat footprints are preserved. PNGs are the source assets; there is no Vallout sprite or audio generator.

Packing: crop reviewed atlas cells, downsample to native frame dimensions, align sprite feet, retain transparent alpha. Actor atlas contains an extra back-facing idle column; discard that duplicate before packing the eleven gameplay poses. Terrain swatches are mapped onto exact 32×16 isometric diamonds. Portraits are 64×64; title is 640×480; map is 612×346; casing is a 64×64 tile. Small cursor/LED/FX/terminal glyphs are pixel-drawn for legibility.

The world map is fictional gameplay cartography, not a navigational map of the real city. Music and sound effects remain owner-supplied assets.
